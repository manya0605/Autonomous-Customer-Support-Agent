NETWORK TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

CATEGORY: Internet Connectivity

SYMPTOM: Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting

DIAGNOSTIC STEPS:

STEP 1:
Restart the Wi-Fi router and reconnect the device.

STEP 2:
Forget the Wi-Fi network on the device and reconnect using the correct password.

STEP 3:
Check whether other devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network also disconnect.

STEP 4:
Move closer to the router and test the connection.

STEP 5:
Check whether the router's Internet/WAN indicator shows an error.

STEP 6:
Restart the affected device.

STEP 7:
If all devices experience the problem, contact the Internet Service Provider or technical support.

ESCALATION:
Escalate when:
- Multiple devices cannot connect.
- The Internet/WAN connection is unavailable.
- The issue continues after restarting the router.
- Hardware failure is suspected.
