REFUND POLICY

Policy Version: 1.0

1. GENERAL REFUND POLICY

Customers may be eligible for a refund when an order qualifies under the company's return, cancellation, or service-failure policies.

Refund decisions must be based on the applicable order status, payment status, return status, and company policy.

2. ELIGIBLE REFUND CASES

A customer may be eligible for a refund when:

- The order was cancelled before shipment.
- The customer successfully returned an eligible product.
- The product was defective or damaged on arrival.
- The customer received the wrong product.
- The company confirms a significant delivery failure.
- A payment was successfully processed but the order was cancelled by the company.

3. NON-ELIGIBLE CASES

A refund may be denied when:

- The product is outside the applicable return period.
- The product is damaged due to customer misuse.
- The customer cannot provide sufficient order information.
- The product belongs to a non-refundable category.
- The payment was never successfully processed.

4. REFUND PROCESS

Once a refund is approved:

- The refund request is recorded.
- The original payment method is used whenever possible.
- The customer receives confirmation of the refund request.
- The final refund processing time depends on the payment provider.

5. IMPORTANT AGENT RULE

The AI support agent must never guarantee a refund unless the order and applicable policy confirm eligibility.

If eligibility cannot be determined automatically, the agent must escalate the request to a human support representative.
