LAPTOP TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

DEVICE: Laptop
CATEGORY: Performance

SYMPTOM: Laptop is very slow

COMMON CAUSES:
- Too many background applications
- Insufficient available memory
- Low disk space
- Startup applications
- Malware or unwanted software
- Overheating
- Outdated drivers

DIAGNOSTIC STEPS:

STEP 1:
Restart the laptop and check whether performance improves.

STEP 2:
Close unnecessary applications running in the background.

STEP 3:
Check available storage space.
Free storage if the system drive is almost full.

STEP 4:
Check startup applications and disable unnecessary programs.

STEP 5:
Check whether the laptop is overheating.
Ensure that air vents are not blocked.

STEP 6:
Install available operating system and driver updates.

STEP 7:
Run a trusted security scan if suspicious software is suspected.

ESCALATION:
Escalate to human technical support when:
- Performance remains poor after troubleshooting.
- The laptop repeatedly shuts down.
- The device overheats significantly.
- Hardware failure is suspected.
- The customer cannot perform the recommended steps.
