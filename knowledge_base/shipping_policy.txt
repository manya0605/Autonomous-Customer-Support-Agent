SHIPPING POLICY

Policy Version: 1.0

1. GENERAL SHIPPING POLICY

Orders are processed and shipped according to product availability, destination, and selected delivery service.

Estimated delivery times are estimates and may change because of operational or external factors.

2. SHIPPING DELAYS

An order may be delayed because of:

- High shipping volume
- Weather conditions
- Transportation problems
- Incorrect or incomplete address
- Regional delivery restrictions
- Warehouse processing delays
- Unexpected logistics issues

3. DELAYED ORDERS

When an order is delayed, the support agent should:

1. Verify the order ID.
2. Check the current order status.
3. Inform the customer of the available status.
4. Avoid promising an exact delivery date unless confirmed by the system.

4. CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION

The agent should acknowledge customer frustration when an order is delayed.

The agent should provide only information available from trusted system data.

5. ESCALATION

A delayed order should be escalated when:

- The customer reports a serious delivery issue.
- The order remains unresolved for an extended period.
- The customer requests human assistance.
- The system cannot determine the delivery status.

6. IMPORTANT AGENT RULE

The AI agent must never invent a tracking update or delivery date.
