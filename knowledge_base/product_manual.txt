PRODUCT MANUAL

1. GENERAL PRODUCT INFORMATION

This product manual contains general setup, usage, maintenance, and troubleshooting information for supported company products.

Customers should follow the instructions applicable to their specific product model.

2. PRODUCT SETUP

Before using a product:

- Remove the product from its packaging.
- Check that all required accessories are included.
- Read the safety instructions.
- Connect the product according to the manufacturer's instructions.
- Complete the initial setup procedure.
- Test the product before normal use.

3. CONNECTIVITY

For products that support Wi-Fi or Bluetooth:

- Enable the required connectivity feature.
- Put the product into pairing or setup mode.
- Open the connectivity settings on the customer device.
- Select the product from the available devices.
- Complete the pairing or setup process.

If the product does not appear:

- Restart the product.
- Restart Bluetooth or Wi-Fi on the customer device.
- Move the devices closer together.
- Remove an old pairing and try again.
- Check whether the product supports the customer's device.

4. PRODUCT NOT WORKING

If a product does not work:

STEP 1:
Check that the product is powered on.

STEP 2:
Check the power cable, charger, battery, or other required connection.

STEP 3:
Restart the product.

STEP 4:
Check whether the product displays an error indicator or message.

STEP 5:
Check the relevant troubleshooting section of this manual.

STEP 6:
If available, check for official firmware or software updates.

5. PRODUCT PERFORMANCE PROBLEMS

If product performance is poor:

- Restart the product.
- Check available power or battery level.
- Check network or connectivity status when applicable.
- Close unnecessary connected applications.
- Check whether the problem occurs consistently.
- Check the product's official troubleshooting instructions.

6. MAINTENANCE

Customers should:

- Keep the product clean.
- Keep ventilation openings unobstructed.
- Use compatible accessories.
- Store the product according to the manufacturer's instructions.
- Avoid exposure to liquids unless the product is specifically designed for it.
- Follow the recommended maintenance schedule.

7. SAFETY

Customers should not:

- Open electrical equipment unless authorized and qualified.
- Modify internal hardware.
- Use damaged power cables.
- Use incompatible chargers or accessories.
- Continue using a product that shows signs of serious electrical or physical damage.

Safety-related problems should be escalated to qualified human support.

8. WARRANTY

Warranty eligibility depends on the applicable product warranty and company policy.

The support agent must verify the applicable warranty information before promising a repair, replacement, or refund.

9. TROUBLESHOOTING ESCALATION

Escalate to human technical support when:

- Standard troubleshooting does not resolve the problem.
- Hardware damage is suspected.
- The product repeatedly fails.
- The customer reports a safety issue.
- The required procedure is unavailable.
- The system cannot determine the correct troubleshooting procedure.
- The customer explicitly requests human assistance.

10. IMPORTANT AGENT RULE

The AI support agent must not invent product specifications, compatibility information, warranty terms, or technical procedures.

When product-specific information is required, the agent should retrieve the relevant product documentation from the company knowledge base.

If reliable information cannot be retrieved, the issue should be escalated to human support.
