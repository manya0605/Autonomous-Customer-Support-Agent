CANCELLATION POLICY

Policy Version: 1.0

1. GENERAL CANCELLATION POLICY

Customers may request cancellation of an order before it reaches a stage where cancellation is no longer possible.

Cancellation availability depends on the current order status.

2. CANCELLATION ELIGIBILITY

Cancellation may be possible when:

- The order is pending.
- The order has not entered shipment processing.
- The company has not completed fulfillment.

Cancellation may not be possible when:

- The order has already been shipped.
- The order has already been delivered.
- The order is already being processed for fulfillment.

3. PAYMENT AND CANCELLATION

If an order is successfully cancelled after payment:

- The payment record should be checked.
- A refund may be initiated according to the refund policy.
- The customer should receive confirmation.

4. IMPORTANT AGENT RULE

The AI agent must check the current order status before claiming that an order can be cancelled.

The agent must never claim that a cancellation or refund has been completed unless the corresponding action has actually succeeded.

5. HUMAN ESCALATION

Cancellation requests should be escalated when:

- The order state is unclear.
- The system cannot determine eligibility.
- The customer disputes the cancellation result.
- A payment-related problem occurs.
